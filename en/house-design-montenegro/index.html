House and villa design in Montenegro

We design residential and commercial buildings to the urban-technical conditions (UTU) and the municipal plan. First we confirm what your plot actually allows — number of floors, coverage and density ratios, building line — and only then start drawing.

We prepare the concept design (idejno rješenje) for the chief city architect’s approval and the main design (glavni projekat) for filing construction. A licensed engineer signs the documents and an authorised firm carries out the review. We talk to you in English and to the authorities in Montenegrin.

Common mistakes and rejection reasons

  1. Coverage or density ratio exceeded
  2. Building crosses the building line
  3. Façade ignores plan guidelines (roof, materials, colours)
  4. Missing area schedule or parking spaces
  5. Design not matched to the survey

FAQ

How much does a house design cost in Montenegro?

It depends on floor area and stage. The calculator gives a ballpark; we confirm a fixed price after reviewing the UTU and your brief.

How long does chief architect approval take?

Usually 15–30 days. We reduce the risk of rejection by checking the design against the plan guidelines first.

Can I order a design while abroad?

Yes. Contract, approvals and delivery are all online; a surveyor or partner visits the plot.

Who signs the design?

A licensed engineer of the relevant discipline. The main design is reviewed by an authorised review firm.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes — alterations, extensions, change of use and single drawings.
