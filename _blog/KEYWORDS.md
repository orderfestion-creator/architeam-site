# Architeam keyword plan

Source: the owner's keyword list (`_blog/keywords-raw.txt`, 313 Montenegrin phrases, 2026-10-26).
**One article per cluster (one search intent), never one article per phrase.** Google understands variants; near-duplicate pages compete with each other and look like scaled content.

Language rotation (decided by the owner: ME more often, RU/EN less): the n-th article overall follows the pattern **ME, ME, RU, ME, ME, EN** (repeat). Count published rows in `_blog/LOG.md` to know which language comes next (0 published → ME, 2 published → RU, 5 published → EN, …).
- ME runs: take the first `todo` cluster in section C.
- RU / EN runs: take the first `todo` topic in section D for that language.

Every run: primary phrase → title, H1, slug, first paragraph, META keyword. Secondary phrases → naturally in headings/body/FAQ, never forced. After publishing set the status to `done YYYY-MM-DD <url>`; if a topic turns out too close to a published article, set `merged into <slug>` and take the next.
